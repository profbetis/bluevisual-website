Hi! I'm Kevin. I specialize in parametric and procedural systems for 3D, typically in Houdini. I love to learn and share knowledge with the people around me, and work hard to output quality material. In my spare time I like to do DIY projects, programming, being out in nature, and enjoy playing (or making!) video games.

I find a lot of creative inspiration from the physical world around me, as well as a way to intimately understand the processes and effects I am often tasked with simulating, controlling, and extending into a production environment.
